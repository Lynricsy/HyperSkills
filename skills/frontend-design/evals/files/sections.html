002 · THE HARDWARE

Spun from one disc of steel

Each of these is a process we run today, not a roadmap promise. The list will stay short on purpose.

003 · SPINNING

One disc, no seams

A seamless body has nowhere to fail, so the vessel outlasts the joint that a pressed pan would have had.

004 · BRAZING

Handles brazed, never spot-welded

Brass flows into the joint at 870C and stays there, which is why the handle is the last part of the pan to loosen.

005 · TEMPERING

Tempered twice, tested cold

Two cycles instead of one, then a cold-shock test that rejects about one body in nine.

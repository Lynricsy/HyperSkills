# FIN-418 — cost reporting from the BigQuery billing export

Owner: Tomasz (platform). Requested by: finance (Ines).

## What finance asked for

1. A net cost per project for October 2026 that matches the invoice.
2. The same figure for every month since May 2026, so they can see the trend before the
   budget review on 20 November.

## Status

- Standard usage cost export was enabled on 2026-10-03 into a new dataset `finops_eu`, created
  in `europe-west1` because that is where the rest of our data lives.
- `net_cost.sql` runs. Its October net cost is about 31% higher than the invoice for the two
  projects on committed-use discounts, and three small projects (which only have free-tier
  usage) are missing from the result entirely.
- Nothing older than 2026-10-03 is in the table yet. I assume the export backfills history
  in the background over a few days, so the May–September months should show up soon.

## Plan

- Fix the sign issue somehow (maybe credits should be added? not sure).
- If the backfill has not arrived by Friday, disable and re-enable the export to kick it.
- Wire the query into a scheduled query that pages us when daily spend jumps, so we catch
  runaway costs the same day.
